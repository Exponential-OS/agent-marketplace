Competitor Difference

CAT 2
Open claw
Build-vs-Integrate Decision: OpenClaw could theoretically be a transport layer for Career Intelligence (execute actions over WhatsApp), but Career Intelligence's philosophy requires file-first local persistence, not messaging-first. Not a natural fit—would require wrapping.


Nano claw

Build-vs-Integrate Decision: NanoClaw's containerization and security posture are superior to OpenClaw for enterprise use. If Career Intelligence needs to run actions at scale (e.g., enterprise deployment), NanoClaw's security model is worth adopting. But again, it's transport, not the OS itself. Potential integration: Career Intelligence could run inside a NanoClaw agent swarm for enterprise features like "outreach coordination" or "interview panel scheduling."


NemoClaw (NVIDIA)
Key differentiation from Career Intelligence: NemoClaw is enterprise governance infrastructure. Career Intelligence is personal operating system philosophy. Two completely different scales and audiences.
