# Flywheel Amplification Module

## Purpose

Content distribution isn't just about launching new campaigns; it's about compounding the value of past work. This module acts as the "Vortex," revisiting old hubs and spokes to inject new life into them via cross-linking, updates, and comment cascades.

## Capabilities

### 1. Cross-Linking Backward (The 10x Reach Protocol)
When a new campaign launches, this module activates to squeeze more juice from old campaigns:
- **Scan Past Ledgers:** Reads the `campaign-master.md` trackers for the last 3 campaigns.
- **Inject Updates:** Posts a new comment on those old LinkedIn/X/Reddit threads saying, "Follow-up to this — just published [New Link]."
- **Pin Threads:** If on X, adds the new thread as a reply to the master pinned thread.

### 2. Reactivating Engagement
- Identifies old posts that historically had high engagement (via the Analytics Engine).
- Adds an "Update:" or "Pull-quote" comment to those high-signal posts weeks later to bump them back into the algorithmic feed.

### 3. Orchestration Support
This module runs synchronously with the Master Orchestrator. While platform modules push the *new* content, this module handles the *backward integration* of the new content into the historical graph.
